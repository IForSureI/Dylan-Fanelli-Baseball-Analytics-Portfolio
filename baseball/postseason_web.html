Hover any player, club or pitch for the breakdown. On a phone, tap. Every chart also has a table.

Method

  • Data: Baseball Savant pitch-level search for 2026 game types F, D, L and W, pulled one day at a time; names, the schedule and starters' lines from the MLB Stats API and the official box scores.
  • WPA is Savant's win expectancy, summed over a plate appearance's pitches and credited to the batter and to the pitcher who threw the last pitch. Changes between pitches (pickoffs, caught stealings) aren't credited to anyone.
  • xwOBA uses Savant's expected wOBA on balls in play and the actual value on walks, hit-by-pitches and strikeouts. Zone = Savant zones 1-9. Whiff rate = swinging strikes and foul tips per swing. Hard-hit = 95+ mph. Barrel = Savant's barrel class.
  • Plate appearances cut short by the end of an inning are left out; automatic balls are left out of pitch counts. Small samples by design: these describe October, they don't project 2027.
  • Every headline number on this page is checked against the published charts' findings.json when the page is built. Code: baseball/postseason_web.py. Static charts and write-up: reports/2026-postseason-snippets.
