The research

Questions, answers, and the evidence for each

Evaluation, original study

Does a pitch wear out on a hitter?

Yes, and it's specific to the pitch type. An earlier changeup or splitter a hitter saw from the pitcher in earlier plate appearances today, in place of a pitch of another type, goes with 1.87 fewer whiff points on the next one. Pitches of other types in earlier plate appearances go with slightly more whiffs, not fewer. The effect carries across games this season and partly to the next same-handed pitcher's changeup. It holds in each of the three seasons, and with platoon and month-level fixed effects.

Should that change when the starter comes out?

No. Costs were fit on 2024–25 and tested on 2026. The per-pitch cost replicates, but it doesn't reach plate-appearance run value, and managers show no detectable response to it. Evaluation, pre-registered

Do hitters adapt to shapes they've seen from other pitchers?

The planned test can't say, and the report leads with that. The plan was committed before any fit and 2026 was held out. The raw association replicated, then failed its own placebo: shapes a hitter is about to see predict his whiffs too, which points to who plays against what. An exploratory follow-up that holds his season-long diet fixed finds a small recent effect, about a quarter of a whiff point per SD, worth about 2 runs a season at most. Don't reorder a bullpen on it.

Evaluation, the analyst's reference

Which numbers can you trust, and when?

The Reliability Atlas covers 29 metrics in 157 groups. For each, it measures how fast the metric becomes signal, how much carries into next season, how much true talent moves within a season, and how far to regress it, with a regress() function that returns intervals. Regressing beat raw numbers out of sample for 135 of 147 groups, and a clean 2024 → 2025 replication agrees. Pitch physics are regressed by games, not pitches: one 100-pitch outing is one look.

Player development

Which pitch-design changes stick and pay?

Four-seam ride is the best-supported lever. It's worth +1.62 whiff points per 2 inches, and the gain's point estimate held into year two. Velocity's payoff fails a placebo, so recovery from a down year isn't ruled out. Slider levers pay but aren't shown to last. Given the shape change, the single change in expected whiff predicts next season's whiff change better than the full lever model, pooled across pitch types. Also built: an individual development plan engine that issues on-track, behind, flat and inconclusive verdicts and checks power when the plan is written.

Trades, front offices

Do front-office ties predict trade partners?

Yes, modestly. Career histories of 115 club decision-makers, every president and GM since 2009 with each stint sourced, were matched to every pair of clubs and season. A pair trades about 14–18% more in seasons when its decision-makers share front-office history, compared with the same pair in other seasons, and the result survives a permutation test and dropping the Angels and Braves. The narrower protégé-meets-old-boss tie is borderline at best. For scale, division rivalry is −45%.

Advance scouting

How should a club pitch the Rays' lineup in the ALCS?

In the zone, for weak contact. The nine regulars missed on 19.9% of their swings against a league rate of 25.1%, and their contact was soft (.331 xwOBA, league .362). Hitter evaluation

Do good swing decisions last, and what is Gleyber Torres worth?

They last, and Torres is worth two years at $28-32M. Decision value repeats year to year (r = 0.80 from 2025 to 2026). How the work is held up

The standard

  • Out of sample by default. Forecasts are tested on seasons their parameters never saw, against a regressed baseline.
  • One evidence standard. Confirmed means p < 0.05 with enough sample. Benjamini–Hochberg is applied when many tests run at once. "No sign of an effect" is claimed only when the interval rules out effects that matter.
  • Pooling and regression rules. Seasons are pooled only with standard errors clustered by pitcher, and small samples are regressed before they're compared.
  • Placebos and matched controls guard against regression to the mean and selection.
  • Plans written down first. The shape-exposure study committed its analysis plan before any fit and held out 2026; when the planned test failed its placebo, the report led with it.
  • Public data only. Statcast via Baseball Savant, MLB Stats API transactions, Baseball-Reference and Retrosheet, plus cited, dated public reporting for contracts, transactions and executive careers.
