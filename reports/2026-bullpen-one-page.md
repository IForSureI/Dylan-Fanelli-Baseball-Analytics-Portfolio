# Rangers bullpen, 2026-27: sign Adrián Morejón

*To: the general manager. From: Dylan Fanelli. Oct. 18, 2026. The one-page version of the [full memo](2026-bullpen-acquisition-memo.md); every number below is derived there.*

**The call.** Bid on Adrián Morejón. Open at four years, $60M, with a 2031 club option. Walk away above four years, $72M. If the Padres make him a qualifying offer, $60M is the walk-away: the second-round pick and pool money it costs us are worth about $10.6M.

**Why him.**
- **The need:** late-inning run prevention. Texas relievers fell from 4th to 24th in run value per 100 pitches, and the pitchers leaving threw 35% of the late, close plate appearances.
- **The pitcher:** a 28-year-old left-hander with a 99.5 mph sinker and a 92 mph changeup that got whiffs on 56% of swings. 2.83 ERA and 30.4% strikeouts over a career-high 86 innings, with 25 multi-inning outings.
- **The projection:** the most 2027 wins of any free-agent reliever.

**The alternative.** Bryan Abreu: open at two years, $18M, walk away above $25M. His fastball came back in the second half of 2026 (95.0 to 96.4 mph) and his walks fell (19.7% to 11.3%, borderline at p = 0.058). Pete Fairbanks is out for 2027 after thoracic outlet surgery.

**The risks.**
- **The elbow:** Tommy John in 2021 and an elbow sprain in 2023. A club medical review gates the offer.
- **What's new:** the 92 mph changeup is one season old, and the sinker gained 1.8 mph. Expect about half the changeup's edge over its physics to carry into 2027.
- **The price:** $72M is about the middle of his likely market. At that number we land him a little less than half the time.

**What would change the call.**
- **A price above $72M, a fifth guaranteed year or an opt-out:** sign Abreu.
- **A relief budget under about $15M a year:** Abreu, or Raisel Iglesias on one year.
- **A decision that 2027 wins near the wild-card line are worth more to us than the market rate:** raise the walk-away. At $80M we land him about 62% of the time.

**The ask.** Approve the offer, the walk-away and the medical review before the qualifying-offer deadline, and aim to sign before the labor agreement expires on Dec. 1. A lockout would stop free-agent talks.
