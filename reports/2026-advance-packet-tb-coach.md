# Rays hitters: the plan

*For the pitchers and catchers. Oct. 18, 2026. Dylan Fanelli. Data: every 2026 regular-season pitch (Statcast); the numbers behind each line are in the [full packet](2026-advance-packet-tb.md). An independent exercise from public data; not prepared for or with the Cleveland Guardians.*

**The lineup in one line.** They put the ball in play, and they don't miss the slider in the dirt like most teams. Throw strikes to the bottom five and make the top three beat you on the edges. A lefty gets the usual edge on their left-handed bats; expect right-handed pinch-hitters when one comes in.

**1. Junior Caminero (R): with a base open, give him nothing to hit**
- **Attack:** take strike one; he lets 3 of every 4 first pitches go. Breaking balls away, changeups down.
- **Avoid:** fastballs up or over the middle, especially under 96. That's his power.
- **Two strikes:** he chases. Bury the breaking ball in the dirt or off the plate away.

**2. Yandy Díaz (R): he won't strike out, so make him hit it on the ground**
- **Attack:** fastballs in on the hands and down. He rolls them over.
- **Avoid:** breaking balls at the belt and anything in the middle.
- **Two strikes:** breaking ball down or a fastball up out of the zone. Expect contact; play the ground ball.

**3. Jonathan Aranda (L): beat him up with velocity**
- **Attack:** he swings at 4 of 10 first pitches. Start him with a changeup away or a fastball at the letters. Fastballs up at 96+ get his misses.
- **Avoid:** the bottom of the zone, down and in, and breaking balls in the zone. He hurts all of it.
- **Two strikes:** fastball above the zone. A breaking ball only if it's in the dirt.

**4. Chandler Simpson (L): strikes, no walks**
- **Attack:** fastballs in the zone, up and in. No power: let him hit it.
- **Avoid:** nibbling. A walk puts his legs on base.
- **Two strikes:** he almost never misses. Stay in the zone with fastballs.

**5. Cedric Mullins (L): didn't start in the ALDS**
- **Attack:** breaking balls for strikes, fastballs at the top of the zone.
- **Avoid:** nothing clear.
- **Two strikes:** breaking ball in the dirt or a fastball above the zone.

**6. Taylor Walls (S)**
- **Attack:** pound the zone; he takes strikes. Changeup away, fastball up.
- **Avoid:** walking him. He has no damage spot, and he walks 1 time in 10.
- **Two strikes:** he won't chase the breaking ball. Finish him in or near the zone: changeup at the knees, fastball just above.

**7. Richie Palacios (L): usually sits against lefties**
- **Attack:** fastballs early, changeups down.
- **Avoid:** nothing clear.
- **Two strikes:** he chases. Breaking ball or changeup in the dirt.

**8. Jonny DeLuca (R): chaser**
- **Attack:** start him with a breaking ball. He swings at breaking balls and changeups off the plate.
- **Avoid:** little stands out; don't leave it down and in.
- **Two strikes:** breaking ball in the dirt, his weakest spot. He catches up to velocity.

**9. Nick Fortes (R): the biggest chaser in the lineup**
- **Attack:** expand from pitch one: fastballs off the plate in or up.
- **Avoid:** nothing stands out. Don't walk him; he walks 4% of the time.
- **Two strikes:** he chases half of them. Fastball up out of the zone, changeup in the dirt.

**Off the bench.** Liam Hicks (L, catching): free strike one, fastballs up; a lefty helps by the usual amount. Ryan Vilade (R, right field against lefties): fast bat, flat swing; fastballs in on the hands, breaking ball in the dirt to finish. Ben Williamson (R): free strike one, expand with two strikes. Victor Mesa Jr. (L): swings early and often; fastball up, breaking ball in the dirt.

*Breaking ball: slider, sweeper or curveball. Changeup includes splitters. Fastball: four-seam or sinker.*
